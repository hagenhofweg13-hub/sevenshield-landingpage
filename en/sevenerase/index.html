Note on SSDs

On SSDs and USB drives, the storage controller distributes writes on its own. Individual old copies therefore cannot be reached in every case. For complete security, store sensitive data encrypted from the start.

That is exactly what SevenShield is for: what was never stored in plain text does not need to be wiped thoroughly later.

SevenErase quick guide

  1. LaunchDouble-click SevenErase.exe. No installation is needed.
  2. SelectDrag files or folders into the list, or add them with the buttons on the right.
  3. ConfigureOne pass (“Durchgänge”: 1) is enough for modern drives. Optionally tick “Danach Freiraum wipen” (wipe free space afterwards).
  4. DeleteClick “Sicher löschen” (delete securely). Progress is shown in the log at the bottom.

“Freiraum wipen” tab: overwrites the free space of a drive, so files that were deleted normally in the past can no longer be recovered either.

“Papierkorb leeren + Freiraum wipen”: empties the recycle bin and wipes free space in one step, for example before passing on a PC.

Deletion report (PDF): after deleting, SevenErase asks whether to save a report. It lists the deleted files and folders, passes, time and result, and carries a checksum. The “Deletion report (PDF)” button saves it later as well. It documents the process but is not a certification.

Right-click menu: the switch at the bottom left adds “Sicher löschen” directly to the File Explorer context menu.

“Metadaten” tab (metadata): removes hidden details such as author, company or editing data from photos, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files and PDFs. “Anzeigen / Bearbeiten” (view / edit) shows a file’s details.

Frequently asked questions about secure deletion

What really happens when you delete files on Windows, and what to watch out for.

Why are deleted files not really gone?

When you delete normally and empty the recycle bin, Windows only removes the reference to the file. The actual data stays on the drive until it is eventually overwritten. Until then, it can be recovered with freely available recovery tools.

How do I permanently delete files before selling my PC?

Delete the files with SevenErase and then wipe the free disk space so that previously deleted remnants disappear too. If you are passing on the whole PC, the Windows feature “Reset this PC” with the option to clean the drive is also useful.

Is one overwrite pass enough?

For modern hard drives, yes. Multiple passes date back to very old drives. One pass with random data is enough to make the data unrecoverable by software.

Does secure deletion also work on SSDs and USB sticks?

Only to a limited extent. The storage controller distributes writes on its own, so individual old copies cannot always be reached. For sensitive data on SSDs, it is safer to store it encrypted from the start, for example with SevenShield.

What do metadata in documents and photos reveal?

Office files and PDFs often store details such as the author, company name, editing times or earlier file names without you noticing, and photos also store camera data and sometimes the location. The “Metadaten” tab in SevenErase removes these details before you share a file.

Is SevenErase really free?

Yes, for deleting files and folders and wiping free disk space: free, with no ads, no sign-up and no installation. Only the deletion report as a PDF (with export for quality management systems) requires a SevenShield or SevenErase license. SevenErase does not transfer any data.

Even safer: never store in plain text

SevenShield encrypts sensitive folders right where they are stored, including file names. What was never on the drive unencrypted does not have to be laboriously deleted later.
